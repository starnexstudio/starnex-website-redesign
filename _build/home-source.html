Frequent questions

What to expect when you work with us, from the first call to launch.

What does StarNex Studio do?

We design and develop custom websites, online stores and web apps, including the structure, interface design, front end and back end.

How does a project start?

With a conversation about your business, your customers and your goals. We then agree the scope, plan and timeline before any design work begins.

How long does a new website take?

It depends on the number of pages, features and how much content is ready. You get a clear timeline in the plan, agreed before work starts.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We start by reviewing what you have, keep what works and rebuild what holds the site back.

Will I be able to edit the site myself?

Yes. Where you need to update content, we set up an editor and walk you through it at handover.
